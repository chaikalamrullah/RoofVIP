## RoofVIP license

RoofVIP is distributed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)** unless a release page explicitly states otherwise.

## Bavarian Open Geodata attribution

The Munich releases use source products provided by the **Bavarian Surveying Administration (Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung)** through Bavarian Open Geodata. For v1.0.3.0 these include:

- DOP20 RGB orthophotos;
- DOM20 digital surface data;
- DGM1 digital terrain data; and
- laser-scanning / LiDAR point-cloud data.

Derived image tiles, elevation crops, and point-cloud subsets retain the applicable source-data attribution requirements.

## RoofVIP-derived products

Manually produced roof annotations, converted polygon representations, derived nDSM data, scene organization, dataset curation, and benchmark structure are distributed as RoofVIP products under **CC BY 4.0**.

Users should attribute both:

1. the **Bavarian Surveying Administration** for the underlying geospatial products; and
2. the **RoofVIP authors** for annotation, processing, curation, and benchmark preparation.

**No endorsement by the original licensors is implied.** Always preserve the source and licensing information included with the archive and consult the corresponding release page before redistribution.
